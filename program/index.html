Program

A practical path from training to trusted community care

Dariva.co develops community coaches through a preventative mental wellness model that builds confidence, leadership, and income pathways.

Overview

The programme turns care into capability.

Through a train-the-trainer approach, Dariva.co equips people to learn, facilitate, mentor, and support others with clear boundaries and continuous support.

Mental wellness foundations

Emotional literacy, stress, trauma awareness, and everyday support practices.

End Gender-Based Violence

Understanding drivers, warning signs, safer conversations, and referral pathways.

Leadership and communication

Facilitation, trust-building, confidentiality, and community presence.

The 3 phases

Learn. Practice. Earn impact.

Each phase deepens skill, responsibility, and real-world contribution.

Phase 01

Learn & Grow

Build emotional literacy, GBV awareness, leadership confidence, and the basics of preventative mental wellness.

Phase 02

Practice & Lead

Facilitate guided sessions, support peers, strengthen relationships, and learn when to refer people for additional care.

Phase 03

Earn & Impact

Serve through digital and community channels, receive mentorship, and grow a dignified pathway in care work.
