The Challenge

Mental wellness should not be a privilege.

In many Namibian communities, professional mental health support is scarce and stigma runs deep. Dariva.co exists to close that gap.

Mental Health Gap

In Namibia, Africa, access to professional mental health support is limited. Community-based prevention is the most practical path to wider wellness.

Silent Suffering

Stigma, isolation, and lack of emotional vocabulary prevent people from speaking about pain early — when care is most effective.

Limited Awareness

Many communities lack access to basic emotional literacy, stress management, and referral knowledge that could prevent crisis.

Fragmented Support

Without trained local coaches and connected referral pathways, people fall through the gaps between informal care and professional help.

Our Solution

A community-owned model for mental wellness.

Dariva.co combines training, digital access, and economic opportunity into one integrated system — making prevention practical and sustainable.

Train

We train community coaches in mental wellness foundations, GBV awareness, facilitation, and ethical care practices.

Connect

Digital tools link trained coaches, community members, records, referrals, and continuous learning into one accessible system.

Reward

An incentive-based model creates dignified income pathways for care work while keeping prevention and trust at the centre.

How It Works

From learning to leading in your community.

Four stages that turn motivation into capable, supported community care.

Step 01

Learn

Community members train in mental wellness foundations, emotional literacy, GBV awareness, safeguarding, and referral skills.

Step 02

Practice

Graduates facilitate peer sessions, build trust, strengthen relationships, and learn when to refer people for additional care.

Step 03

Serve

Trained coaches serve their communities through digital and in-person channels, supported by ongoing mentorship and supervision.

Step 04

Lead

Care work becomes a dignified pathway to income, leadership, and lasting community impact — creating a self-sustaining wellness ecosystem.
